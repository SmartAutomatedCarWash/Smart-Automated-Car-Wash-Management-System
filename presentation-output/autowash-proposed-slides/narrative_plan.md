Accuracy boundaries: SePay QR and VNPay are payment mechanisms. Staff-assisted check-in uses booking data or vehicle plate search; the repository does not implement a hardware QR gate/scanner workflow. Loyalty points are awarded after completed wash sessions, and customers redeem points for eligible vouchers.

Visual system: white board on light gray background, black typography, purple and cyan accent geometry, editable icons built from native PowerPoint shapes.

Editability plan: all visible text, diagrams, cards, arrows, and icons are native editable PowerPoint objects.
