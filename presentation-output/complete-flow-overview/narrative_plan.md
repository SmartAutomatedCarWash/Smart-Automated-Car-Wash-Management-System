# AutoWash Complete Functional Flow Overview

- Audience: lecturers, reviewers, and project presentation attendees.
- Objective: present all major and distinctive business flows of the AutoWash project without technical implementation details.
- Narrative: begin with the complete system journey, then explain account and vehicle preparation, booking and payment, staff and car-wash operations, loyalty offers, customer feedback, and management reporting.
- Slide list:
  1. Overall Functional Flow
  2. Account & Vehicle Preparation Flow
  3. Service Discovery, Booking & Payment Flow
  4. Staff Assignment & Car Wash Flow
  5. Combo, Promotion & Loyalty Flow
  6. Review, History & Customer Retention Flow
  7. Management & Reporting Flow
- Visual system: white canvas, strong colored scenario title, large pictograms, short action labels, continuous snake arrows, and one plain-language rule per slide.
- Editability: all titles, steps, arrows, callouts, and labels are editable PowerPoint objects.
