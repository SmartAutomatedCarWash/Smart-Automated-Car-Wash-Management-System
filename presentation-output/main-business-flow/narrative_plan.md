# AutoWash Main Business Flow

## Audience
Project evaluation panel, lecturers, product stakeholders, and implementation team.

## Objective
Explain the complete core business journey without separating the process by system role and without exposing implementation-oriented API language.

## Narrative arc
1. Present one end-to-end booking, service, and aftercare flow.
2. Expand the booking, capacity, validation, and payment decisions.
3. Expand service execution, completion, loyalty, and review decisions.

## Slide list
1. End-to-End Main Business Flow
2. Booking and Payment Decision Flow
3. Service Execution and Aftercare Flow

## Source plan
Business rules are aligned with the implementation on `origin/dev` at commit `b5ad575`.

## Visual system
Professional ISO-style flowchart language: rounded terminators for start/end, rectangles for processes, diamonds for decisions, solid directional arrows for the main path, and labeled Yes/No branches for alternatives and loops.

## Editability plan
All text, flowchart symbols, status labels, branch labels, phase bands, and arrows remain editable PowerPoint objects.
