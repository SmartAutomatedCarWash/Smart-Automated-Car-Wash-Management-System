# AutoWash Main Flow Overview

- Audience: lecturers, reviewers, and project presentation attendees.
- Objective: summarize the project's main business journey on one slide without technical implementation details.
- Narrative: customer discovers a service, prepares a vehicle and schedule, confirms a booking and payment, receives the car wash service, then earns loyalty benefits and leaves feedback.
- Slide list: one overview flow slide with eight main steps and one supporting management band.
- Visual system: white presentation canvas, orange section accent, cyan/green/purple process colors, large pictograms, short labels, and connected arrows.
- Editability: titles, labels, arrows, process nodes, and management support cards remain editable PowerPoint objects.
