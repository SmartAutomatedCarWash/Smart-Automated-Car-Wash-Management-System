# AutoWash Project Flow Scenarios

## Audience
Project evaluation panel, lecturers, product stakeholders, and development team.

## Objective
Explain the complete repository-aligned user and operational journey using visual scenario flows similar to the supplied presentation sample.

## Narrative arc
The deck follows a booking from account onboarding through service discovery, slot reservation, payment, staff operations, realtime tracking, completion, loyalty, review, and management reporting.

## Slide list
1. Account Registration, Authentication and Vehicle Setup
2. Service Discovery, Vehicle Selection and Slot Availability
3. Voucher, Staff Preference and Booking Creation
4. Online Payment and Booking Confirmation
5. Staff Assignment, Queue and Check-in
6. Wash Progress and Realtime Customer Tracking
7. Completion, Loyalty, Tier and Review
8. Booking History, Administration and Reporting

## Source plan
Use endpoints, states, and business rules implemented on `origin/dev` at commit `b5ad575`.

## Visual system
White 16:9 canvas matching the sample; black section title, orange scenario title, editable mini interface cards, numbered steps, and a left-to-right then right-to-left snake flow. Cyan identifies frontend actions, purple identifies backend commands, green identifies persistence or successful state, and orange identifies integrations or payment.

## Editability plan
All visible text, nodes, arrows, status labels, endpoint references, and rules remain editable PowerPoint objects.
